# From question to result

1. State a precise question and its scope in the branch's experiments directory. Identify the surface or family, equivalence relation and assumptions.
2. Cite definitions and prior results by exact locator. Mark an unproved expectation as a **conjecture**. Mark an input condition used without proof as an **assumption**.
3. Describe the algorithm, the predicted observable and the criterion that could refute the prediction. For a finite search, specify the domain and the argument for exhaustiveness.
4. Record what the engine produced as a **computation**, with the [reproduction record](reproducibility.md). Preserve contradictory outputs and identify follow-up work.
5. State a **proved result** with a proof or a cited theorem whose hypotheses have been checked. For a computer-assisted proof, explain why exact verification of the attached output implies the stated conclusion.

Link a report to its experiment, data and verification evidence. Update the roadmap or changelog when the state of a research objective changes.
