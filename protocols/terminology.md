# Terminology

Use the [shared definitions](../shared/definitions/surfaces.md) for the families and their invariants. Specify the equivalence relation when counting or identifying surfaces, for example biholomorphism, conjugacy of lattices or commensurability. Give the base field when it matters for equivalence.

Use *covering* with its category and hypotheses. For a finite map, identify the source, target, degree and branch locus when known. Supply the uniformizing domain and the action when calling a surface a quotient. Qualify a statement about an irreducible arithmetic subgroup with that hypothesis.

Use *classification label* for a designation within a named source and *surface ID* for the local registry key. State whether a label identifies an individual surface, a conjugate pair, a lattice or a class.
